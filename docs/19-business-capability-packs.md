# 19. Business Capability Packs

Capability packs let an owner select useful outcomes without installing every integration.

## Core operator

Included by default:

1. Natural conversational intake
2. Working stack and save points
3. Current correction precedence
4. Selective memory
5. Source backed decisions
6. Evidence based completion
7. Temporary specialist delegation
8. Learning through reviewed skills
9. Native Hermes Kanban as the canonical task list
10. Manual unassigned cards that never dispatch
11. Automatic reconciliation from conversations, meetings, email, calendar, files, CRM, and automation evidence

## Executive assistant

1. Inbox triage
2. Email drafting and approved sending
3. Calendar management
4. Meeting preparation
5. Transcript and action extraction
6. Reminders and follow ups
7. Daily and weekly briefs
8. Relationship context
9. Document and spreadsheet production
10. Notification routing

## CRM and relationships

1. People and companies
2. Opportunities and pipeline
3. Projects and tasks
4. Notes and communication history
5. Meeting and email logging
6. Natural language questions
7. Views and dashboards
8. Workflow automation
9. Deduplication and data quality
10. Migration and cleanup

Twenty is the recommended open source reference. Existing CRMs can use the same contract.

## Growth intelligence

1. Advertising analysis
2. Analytics and attribution
3. Calls, leads, bookings, and revenue reconciliation
4. SEO and keyword research
5. Search result and competitor analysis
6. Local business data
7. Website and content review
8. Recurring reports
9. Tracking diagnostics
10. Staged recommendations

DataForSEO, Google services, crawlers, direct advertising APIs, and aggregators are examples, not requirements.

## Public relations and outreach

1. Opportunity monitoring
2. Qualification against saved criteria
3. Publication and contact research
4. Pitch drafting in an onboarded voice
5. Approval queue
6. Sent record and reply tracking
7. CRM logging
8. Follow up
9. Win notification
10. Background silence when nothing matters

The bundled skill provides the operating pattern without distributing a private application.

## Documents and content

2. Excel and CSV
3. PDF and OCR
4. Presentations

6. Research with citations
7. Contract obligations

9. Knowledge base maintenance

## Artifacts and cloud storage

1. Local files and shared folders
2. Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, and Box adapters
3. S3-compatible buckets and approved object stores
4. Stable artifact identity and version history
5. Checksum and provider readback
6. Visibility, retention, and revocation controls
7. Temporary non-sensitive previews through nip.io or sslip.io when appropriate

Provider accounts and access policies are selected during onboarding.

## Optional connector layer

Composio can be selected for supported SaaS integrations, including common sheets and storage providers. Direct APIs, native Hermes integrations, and provider MCP servers remain equally valid. Current scope, account identity, privacy, retention, usage limits, cost, revocation, and fallback must pass before activation.

## Selection rule

Do not install a capability because it sounds useful. Enable it when:

1. The owner names a real outcome.
2. The source and data boundary are known.
3. A provider or manual source exists.
4. Approval rules are defined.
5. The success test is clear.
6. The maintenance cost is acceptable.

Unused packs stay disabled.
